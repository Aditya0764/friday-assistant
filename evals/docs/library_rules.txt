Lakeview Public Library Rules

Lakeview Public Library opened in 1998 and has about 24000 books. It is open Monday to Saturday from 9 AM to 7 PM and is closed on Sundays. The head librarian is Mrs. Kavitha Reddy.

Members can borrow up to 4 books at a time for 21 days. A book can be renewed twice, unless another member has reserved it. Late fees are 2 rupees per day per book, capped at 100 rupees. A lost book must be paid for at its full price plus a 50 rupee handling fee.

Membership costs 300 rupees per year. Children under 12 join for free.

The reading room on the second floor has 40 seats, and silence is required. Laptops are allowed only in the reading room. Photocopying costs 1 rupee per page. Wi-Fi is available on the ground floor only, and the password is given at the front desk.

The library holds a story hour for children every Saturday at 11 AM.
