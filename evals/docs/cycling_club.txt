Kaveri Cycling Club Handbook

The Kaveri Cycling Club was founded in 2014 by Meera Rao in Mysuru. It has 86 members. The club meets every Sunday at 5:30 AM at the Chamundi Hill parking lot. The standard weekend ride is 42 kilometres and takes about two hours.

Membership costs 1800 rupees per year, and students pay 900 rupees. Every rider must wear a helmet. Riders without one are not allowed to join the group.

The club has three ride groups. Green is for beginners and rides at 18 kilometres per hour. Blue is for intermediate riders and rides at 24 kilometres per hour. Red is for fast riders and rides at 30 kilometres per hour. The ride captain for the Blue group is Arjun Nair.

On the first Saturday of every month, the club does a 100 kilometre ride to Srirangapatna and back. The support van carries water and spare tubes, and it is driven by Imran Sheikh. In case of a puncture, the nearest rider helps fix it, and each rider must carry one spare tube.

The annual club dinner is held in December at the Lalitha Mahal lawn. Membership applications are sent by email to the secretary, Divya Menon.
